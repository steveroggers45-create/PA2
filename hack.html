Your first hackathon? You're in the right place.

A simple guide from idea to demo.

You do not need a finished idea or a perfect team to get started. Bring curiosity; mentors and organizers can help with the rest.

01

Find a real problem

Start with a person and a pain point. Ask who needs your solution and what would make their day easier.

02

Form a balanced team

Teams of 2–4 are proposed. Mix skills where possible: design, research, coding, storytelling, and domain knowledge.

03

Make a small prototype

Focus on one useful feature. Sketch, build, and test the core idea rather than trying to make a complete product.

04

Prepare a clear demo

Explain the problem, show what you built, and share what you learned. Keep your pitch simple and your demo ready.

05

Use tools responsibly

Credit collaborators and sources, respect data privacy, and be transparent about third-party tools and AI use.

06

Ask for help early

Bring blockers to mentors or event staff. Proposed contact: hackathon@northstar.edu (confirm before publishing).
